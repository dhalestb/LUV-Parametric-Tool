## Result

The 12-letter study contains four lower, four middle and four top sources. Two middle sources stand upright at X tilt 90 degrees. Their global Z turns direct through-openings along X and Y. Lower and top sources incline at most 12 degrees.

The arrangement uses the surrounding solids' projected convex hull to keep complete upright source footprints at least 0.8 ft inside the perimeter. Terrace sources reaching the outer hull receive perimeter wall roles. Remaining horizontal sources receive terrace roles. XY placement is sampled and scored from neighboring sources, so the lift assignments do not fix the plan arrangement.

New composition generation targets an overall source height of approximately three preferred letter sizes, constrained by the selected field. In the reviewed 40 × 40 × 60 ft field with 13 ft preferred letters, the source geometry occupies elevations 1.01–37.93 ft. The source bands are lower 1.01–5.24 ft, middle 12.22–27.30 ft and top 33.44–37.93 ft. Their footprints overlap in plan.

Upper and middle terrace bases have local depth capped at 0.45 times the preferred letter size. Exterior wall construction continues vertically to its configured support. Middle through-cutters add no full-height supporting bays. They subtract from the mass generated by surrounding letters. Negative contributions are measured from exposed final cut boundaries using the same ray and exposed-area method as positive components. The 1 ft² visible contribution threshold remains.

Regeneration preserves every submitted source pose. Existing source records use their existing generation behavior. Adding sources preserves the existing source prefix.

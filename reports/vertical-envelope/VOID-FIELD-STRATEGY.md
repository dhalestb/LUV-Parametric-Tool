# Deferred strategy — Void Field Gathering

This is a future typology-specific operation. No inclined void-wall system is implemented by the current Stepped Amphitheater experiment.

1. Preserve immutable source-letter identities, matrices and selected field. Derive concave channel boundaries and a cross-level relationship graph from actual transformed geometry.
2. At explicit elevations, intersect source solids with horizontal planes to obtain source-derived void sections. Use projected concave boundaries, adjacent source tangents, scale changes and offsets between related levels to define the section evolution. Do not introduce arbitrary noise or lean angles.
3. Resolve section correspondence explicitly before lofting: boundary IDs, winding, holes and split/merge topology. A discontinuity or ambiguous upper branch needs a reported design decision; it must not be hidden by stretching a previous section. Interpolate compatible sections through piecewise ruled or smoothly tessellated lofts with a stated geometric error bound.
4. The lofts become subtractive tools. Depending on a configurable Void Field operation, sections can taper, expand or move laterally to produce inclined canyon walls and connected multilevel voids. The inclination belongs to the cutter boundary; it is not a global deformation of floor plates or Stepped Amphitheater exterior walls.
5. Construct floor plates from exact horizontal cross-sections at explicit levels. Subtract the protected continuous void tools after unions and connecting additions so openings remain clear. Connections must route around protected voids or fail; do not plug a canyon to repair connectivity.
6. Validate manifold topology, source correspondence, field containment, opening clearance at every level and between levels, positive wall thickness, no self-intersections, continuity of intended void connections and planar floor surfaces. Geometric connectivity does not establish occupant safety or accessible circulation.
7. Store operation choice, source IDs, section elevations, correspondence decisions, taper/profile parameters and tolerances in the request, cache identity and saved snapshot. Provide an explicit off option and keep this operation unavailable to Stepped Amphitheater by default.

Future architectural choices still requiring design review: which sections are occupied, passage widths/heights, minimum remaining wall thickness, clearance/overhead requirements, structural support, split/merge policy and circulation validation. These are not certified by the present generator.
